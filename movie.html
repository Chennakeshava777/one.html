My Top Favourite Movies

  1. Aakasham

    An introverted man with severe OCD goes on a soul-searching journey across India to find the real-life endings of two unfinished short stories. Along the way, his perspective on life transforms after meeting a vibrant fellow traveler.

  2. Oopiri

    A wealthy quadriplegic billionaire hires a carefree ex-convict on parole to be his full-time caretaker. Despite their completely different backgrounds, they form an unlikely, life-changing bond that teaches them both the true value of relationships over money.

  3. Annamayya

    The film follows the spiritual evolution of a young man whose intense devotion leads him to dedicate his life to singing hymns for Lord Venkateswara. Despite trials, political persecution, and earthly attachments, his timeless compositions elevate him to eternal sainthood

  4. Charlie

    A lonely, reclusive factory worker named Dharma finds his life transformed after he rescues an abused and energetic Labrador pup named Charlie. The duo embarks on a heartwarming journey across India that leads to a new perspective on life and redemption.

  5. 3

    The film follows the life of high school sweethearts Ram and Janani as they grow up and eventually marry, but their happiness is shattered when Ram suddenly commits suicide. Janani must then embark on an emotional journey to uncover the hidden psychological truth behind his mysterious death.
